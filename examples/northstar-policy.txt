Northstar Research — Synthetic Travel Handbook

This is a fictional policy created solely for an installation check.

Northstar Research reimburses overnight business travel meals up to 2,450 Indian
rupees per employee per day. This daily meal reimbursement limit includes
breakfast, lunch, dinner, and non-alcoholic drinks. Employees must submit itemized
receipts within fourteen calendar days after returning from a business trip.

Hotel accommodation has a separate limit of 8,000 Indian rupees per night.
Employees need written approval from their team manager before booking travel.
Personal holidays and alcoholic drinks are not reimbursable business expenses.
